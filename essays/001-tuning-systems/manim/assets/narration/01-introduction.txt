The human ear can perceive frequencies between twenty hertz and twenty thousand hertz.

In music, each note we hear is a pitched sound, but there are relatively few.
A grand piano, for instance, has eighty eight keys, with the lowest vibrating at twenty seven point five hertz and the highest at four thousand one hundred eighty six hertz.
So how do we choose the specific frequencies that will make up the notes in our music?
